# Urava Adapter — StoryBrand

Wraps upstream `storybrand/SKILL.md` for Urava messaging architecture.

## Core behaviors retained

- Position the customer as the protagonist and Urava/product as the guide.
- Clarify one primary customer desire.
- Define the external, internal and philosophical dimensions of the problem where evidence supports them.
- Establish the guide with empathy and authority.
- Give a short plan and one clear direct CTA; use a transitional CTA only when useful.
- Make stakes and success explicit without drowning the message in company-centric detail.

## Urava additions

1. Story structure cannot invent customer pain, proof, authority, urgency or transformation. Every material claim must trace to discovery, sales, delivery or approved company evidence.
2. Do not use philosophical-problem language to manufacture outrage. It is allowed only when customers actually frame the issue that way or the claim is independently defensible.
3. Authority assets must be permissioned and real: verified outcomes, testimonials, client logos, credentials or measurable proof.
4. The CTA must match the user's stage and actual operational capacity. Do not send users into a broken or unavailable flow.
5. Website/product copy is jointly reviewed by Product & Requirements and UI/UX for comprehension, hierarchy, mobile usability and trust.
6. If customers cannot understand the offer because positioning is unresolved, route to positioning work rather than forcing a story framework.
7. Message clarity is downstream of demand, offer and sales evidence. It does not compensate for weak fundamentals.

## Output packet

- Customer / primary desire
- External problem
- Internal problem evidence
- Philosophical claim if justified
- Empathy statement
- Authority/proof source
- 3–6 step plan
- Direct CTA
- Transitional CTA if needed
- Stakes
- Success / identity transformation
- Unsupported-claim flags
- Comprehension test

## Handoff

Once the message is truthful, clear and structurally coherent, route to `made-to-stick` only if memorability, attention, belief, caring or action remains weak.
