# Urava Adapter — 100M Leads

Wraps upstream `100m-leads/SKILL.md` for Urava lead-system execution.

## Core behaviors retained

- Distinguish warm outreach, free content, cold outreach and paid ads as the Core Four.
- Master one lead mechanism before adding more.
- Distinguish lead, engaged lead and qualified lead.
- Use lead magnets only when they solve a narrow problem and naturally connect to the core offer.
- Track acquisition economics and improve the current mechanism before adding a new one.
- Add referrals, employees, agencies or affiliates only after Urava can reliably generate leads itself.

## Urava additions

1. `100m-leads` does not decide the acquisition channel by itself when channel uncertainty is the bottleneck; `traction` owns channel selection first.
2. Volume rules such as the Rule of 100 are effort heuristics, not automatic Urava quotas. Capacity, list quality, legal/compliance constraints and evidence determine operating volume.
3. Cold outreach must honor opt-outs, provider terms, applicable communication rules and Urava commercial-integrity standards. Never trade reputation or deliverability for raw volume.
4. Lead scoring must use observable fit/intent evidence and remain explainable; no fabricated qualification.
5. Lead generation is capacity-governed. Do not accelerate qualified lead intake beyond Studio/Apex/Foundry delivery and sales capacity.
6. Track source → engagement → qualification → sales advance → close → payment → delivery/proof so lead volume can be tied to business outcomes.
7. Paid acquisition requires founder approval before spend. Acquisition economics must use real cost and gross-profit/delivery data where available.

## Output packet

- Chosen lead mechanism/channel
- Audience/list definition
- Lead / engaged / qualified state rules
- Lead magnet or outreach offer if used
- Activity volume and capacity limit
- Source, response, qualification and advance metrics
- Acquisition-economics evidence
- Compliance/reputation flags
- More / Better / New decision
- Next execution action

## Handoff

Qualified B2B opportunities with complex need development route to `spin-selling`. Lead volume without conversion routes backward to offer/pricing or qualification evidence rather than increasing volume blindly.
