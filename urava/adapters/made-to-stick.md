# Urava Adapter — Made to Stick

Wraps upstream `made-to-stick/SKILL.md` for Urava communication quality.

## Core behaviors retained

- Use SUCCESs as a diagnostic rather than a requirement to force all six principles into every message.
- Start with **Simple**: identify the one core idea that must survive.
- Use **Unexpected** when attention is weak, **Concrete** when the message is abstract/forgettable, **Credible** when belief is weak, **Emotional** when people understand but do not care, and **Stories** when people care but cannot mentally simulate action.
- Treat the Curse of Knowledge as a standing risk for founders, engineers and experts.

## Urava additions

1. Stickiness comes after truth and clarity. Do not make a weak or unsupported claim more memorable.
2. Surprise must serve the core message; clickbait, bait-and-switch hooks and misleading curiosity gaps are rejected.
3. Credibility must come from real evidence, testable proof, concrete detail or legitimate authority—not inflated statistics or invented examples.
4. Emotional framing cannot exploit fear, shame or identity in a deceptive or coercive way.
5. Stories must be true, clearly hypothetical, or explicitly illustrative. Never fictionalize a customer result and present it as proof.
6. UI/UX reviews the delivery surface so the core message is actually visible and understandable, especially on mobile.
7. Measure comprehension, recall and action where practical; don't assume a clever message worked because the team likes it.

## Output packet

- Audience
- Commander's Intent / one core message
- Current communication symptom
- Broken SUCCESs dimensions
- Concrete rewrite/intervention for only those dimensions
- Proof/credibility source
- Emotional/identity integrity check
- Story source if used
- Comprehension/recall/action test
- Next message experiment

## Handoff

If the audience still does not understand the offer, route back to `storybrand` or positioning. If they understand and remember but do not buy, route to offer, price, sales or channel evidence rather than adding more storytelling.
