# Urava Adapter — 100M Offers

Wraps upstream `100m-offers/SKILL.md` for Urava offer design.

## Core behaviors retained

- Use only after a real target market/problem has been validated.
- Define the desired outcome, identify customer obstacles, turn obstacles into solutions, choose delivery vehicles, trim weak components, and stack the strongest value.
- Use the value equation as a diagnostic: increase desired outcome and perceived likelihood; reduce time delay and customer effort/sacrifice.
- Treat guarantees, scarcity, urgency, naming and price presentation as offer enhancers, not substitutes for demand.

## Urava additions

1. Entry gate requires Mom Test evidence or stronger evidence such as real payments, repeated behavior, LOIs, or equivalent commitment.
2. Every offer component must have both **customer value** and **delivery cost/capacity** recorded; high-value/high-cost components cannot be stacked blindly.
3. No fabricated scarcity, false deadlines, inflated reference values, fake proof, or guarantees Urava cannot operationally honor.
4. Separate a **pricing hypothesis** from a committed market price. Final commercial pricing is founder-gated.
5. If qualified leads exist but conversion is weak, offer design is eligible. If leads are not qualified or the market is uncertain, route backward instead of polishing the offer.
6. Use actual customer language from discovery as the source for outcome/obstacle wording where available.

## Output packet

- Target segment
- Validated desired outcome
- Major obstacles
- Proposed delivery components
- Value-equation weaknesses
- Delivery cost/capacity risks
- Proof/guarantee evidence
- Pricing hypothesis or approved price
- Integrity flags
- Next validation action

## Handoff

Route to `monetizing-innovation` when WTP, tier design, monetization model, or price fences remain unresolved. Route to `traction` only when offer/price is strong enough to test in-market.
