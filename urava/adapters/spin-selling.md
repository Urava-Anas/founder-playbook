# Urava Adapter — SPIN Selling

Wraps upstream `spin-selling/SKILL.md` for Urava's complex B2B sales work.

## Core behaviors retained

- Use SPIN only when the sale is sufficiently complex: multi-call, multiple stakeholders, meaningful buyer risk, or a need to justify the decision internally.
- Keep Situation questions minimal and researchable context out of the call where possible.
- Use Problem questions to surface real difficulties, Implication questions to make consequences concrete, and Need-payoff questions to help the buyer articulate value.
- Do not treat an implied need as a buying signal; develop it into an explicit need.
- Demonstrate benefits only after the customer has expressed an explicit need the solution can actually meet.
- Judge calls by **Order / Advance / Continuation / No-sale**. A vague continuation is not success.

## Urava additions

1. Never use implication questions to manufacture fear or exaggerate consequences. Quantify from customer evidence where possible.
2. Pre-call research should include organisation, role, likely buying process and known operational context so the customer is not forced through avoidable Situation questions.
3. For Apex/Studio deals, separately identify user, economic buyer, operational evaluator and technical/security evaluator when they differ.
4. Every call ends with one highest-realistic **Advance**: a concrete customer action that moves the sale forward. Do not optimize for compliments.
5. Repeated value/price objections are evidence for `100m-offers` or `monetizing-innovation`; they are not automatically objection-handling problems.
6. Sales pressure must never override truthful scope, delivery capacity, security boundaries or customer consent.
7. Record the customer's own explicit-need language for later offer and messaging evidence.

## Output packet

- Stakeholder / role
- Known situation context
- Planned Problem questions
- Planned Implication paths
- Planned Need-payoff questions
- Explicit needs expressed
- Benefits tied to those needs
- Objections / contradictory evidence
- Call outcome
- Specific Advance and owner
- Upstream issue if the sale should route backward

## Handoff

Use recurring customer language and proof from successful sales to strengthen `storybrand`. If the deal stalls because of economics, qualification or the offer, route backward rather than applying more closing pressure.
