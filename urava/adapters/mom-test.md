# Urava Adapter — Mom Test

Wraps upstream `mom-test/SKILL.md` for Urava customer discovery.

## Core behaviors retained

- Ask about the customer's real life and past behavior, not whether they like our idea.
- Replace hypotheticals with the last specific instance.
- Deflect compliments back to current behavior and cost.
- Treat time, reputation and money commitments as stronger evidence than praise.
- Slice broad customer groups when answers diverge instead of averaging incompatible segments.

## Urava additions

1. Record the segment, role, current workaround, cost/time consequence, buying authority and evidence quality in Orbit/approved research records when available.
2. Separate **discovery** from **sales**. Do not disguise a pitch as research.
3. For Apex/Studio B2B discovery, identify user, economic buyer and technical/operational evaluator separately when they differ.
4. Do not convert one enthusiastic interview into roadmap priority. Look for repeated evidence or a strong commitment.
5. After repeated answers become predictable or real payments/LOIs appear, stop endless interviewing and move to build/sales validation.
6. Use customer language as input to positioning/offer work, but keep final claims truthful and evidence-backed.

## Output packet

- Segment / role
- Last specific problem event
- Current workaround
- Quantified consequence if known
- Alternatives already tried
- Budget / buying process signal
- Commitment signal
- Contradictory evidence
- Confidence
- Next learning question or next validation action

## Handoff

Discovery evidence may route next to `100m-offers`, `monetizing-innovation`, `obviously-awesome`, `spin-selling`, or directly to Product & Requirements depending on the actual bottleneck. Do not automatically chain all of them.
