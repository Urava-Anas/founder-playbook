# Developer House ↔ Founder Intelligence Skill Map

This map assigns each upstream business-intelligence module to existing or planned Developer House roles. Modules are reference intelligence, not independent always-on agents.

| Module | Primary Developer House skill | Urava systems/use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| diagnose | Engineering Dispatcher / Product & Requirements | Founder prioritization, commercial triage | Meta-router only; output one primary module. |
| mom-test | Product & Requirements Engineer | Lead discovery, client interviews, Foundry/Studio research | Past behavior over hypotheticals; use Urava adapter. |
| four-steps | Product & Requirements Engineer | New market/customer development | Use when business-model/customer discovery is genuinely unresolved. |
| lean-startup | Product & Requirements + Observability | Product experiments, Orbit/Studio learning loops | Experiments need measurable evidence, not vanity activity. |
| obviously-awesome | Product & Requirements + UI/UX | Positioning, website/product category | Positioning evidence should feed messaging and targeting. |
| crossing-the-chasm | Systems/Product Architect | Market expansion strategy | Not for pre-traction products. |
| blue-ocean-strategy | Systems Architect + Devil's Advocate | Strategic differentiation | Treat as strategy lens, not proof of demand. |
| monetizing-innovation | Product & Requirements + Founder | Pricing/product design | Pricing commitments are founder-gated. |
| spin-selling | Product & Requirements / Commercial execution | Apex and Studio B2B selling | Sales-stage skill, not early discovery. |
| 100m-offers | Product & Requirements + Founder | Urava offers, Studio packages, Foundry admissions | Must obey Urava commercial integrity rules. |
| 100m-leads | Lead/Integration execution + Observability | Orbit Lead Engine, outbound/inbound systems | Tactics require channel metrics and capacity governors. |
| money-models | Founder + Observability | Unit economics, offer sequencing | Financial assumptions require real cash/economic data. |
| influence | Devil's Advocate + Product/Commercial | Ethical persuasion, negotiations, UX trust | Never authorizes deception/dark patterns. |
| traction | Product & Requirements + Observability | Channel tests, Internet HQ | Test channels systematically; avoid parallel channel sprawl. |
| storybrand | UI/UX + Product & Requirements | Website/copy/content flows | Messaging structure; evidence from real user comprehension. |
| made-to-stick | UI/UX + Product/Requirements | Pitches, proposals, briefings, content | Use after message truth is established. |

## Routing principle

Developer House owns execution. Founder Intelligence supplies bounded frameworks. A module should normally be loaded by the Dispatcher or a relevant skill, then discarded from active context after its decision/action packet is produced.
