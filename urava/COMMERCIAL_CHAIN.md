# Urava Commercial Intelligence Chain v1

This is a selective operating sequence for turning uncertain demand into truthful, repeatable commercial execution.

`Mom Test → 100M Offers + Monetizing Innovation → Traction + 100M Leads → SPIN Selling → StoryBrand + Made to Stick`

The arrow means **eligible handoff**, not "load every module." The Dispatcher loads only the stage that matches the current bottleneck.

## Stage 1 — Demand Evidence

**Primary:** `mom-test`

Entry: customer/problem evidence is weak, compliment-heavy, hypothetical, or unclear.

Required output before advancing:
- defined segment and role;
- repeated real problem behavior or a strong commitment signal;
- current workaround and consequence;
- budget/buying-process evidence where relevant;
- contradictory evidence recorded, not averaged away.

Fail/route back: if the problem is not real or the segment is incoherent, do not design an offer.

## Stage 2 — Offer + Monetization

**Primary:** `100m-offers` for packaging/value.  
**Secondary:** `monetizing-innovation` when willingness-to-pay, tiers, monetization model, or price fences are unresolved.

Required output before advancing:
- one validated target segment;
- explicit outcome and major obstacles;
- bounded delivery stack with delivery cost/capacity understood;
- WTP evidence or a clearly labelled pricing hypothesis;
- price/monetization rationale with a customer-evidence "because";
- guarantees, scarcity and urgency pass Urava commercial-integrity rules.

Founder gate: final pricing commitments, paid guarantees with material liability, and spend implications.

Fail/route back: weak demand evidence → Mom Test. Unclear competitive category → positioning skill, not more offer stacking.

## Stage 3 — Channel + Lead System

**Primary:** `traction` to select/test channels.  
**Secondary:** `100m-leads` to operationalize the chosen lead mechanism.

Required output before advancing:
- one traction goal;
- bounded channel tests with cost, volume, lead quality and cycle-time metrics;
- one current focus channel once evidence identifies it;
- defined lead → engaged lead → qualified lead states;
- capacity governor so demand cannot outrun delivery;
- acquisition economics measured where data exists.

Founder gate: paid channel spend and material external campaign commitments.

Fail/route back: leads arrive but do not convert → offer/price. No meaningful demand signal across well-run tests → revisit segment/problem evidence.

## Stage 4 — B2B Need Development + Advance

**Primary:** `spin-selling` when the sale is complex, multi-call, high-risk, or involves multiple stakeholders.

Required output before advancing:
- minimal researched situation context;
- customer-stated problems;
- implications quantified or made concrete where possible;
- explicit need stated by the buyer;
- solution benefit tied only to that explicit need;
- one specific next-step **Advance**, not a vague continuation.

Fail/route back: repeated objection around value/price → offer or monetization. Wrong buyer/segment → demand evidence. Weak lead quality → channel/lead system.

## Stage 5 — Message Clarity + Memory

**Primary:** `storybrand` for message architecture and clarity.  
**Secondary:** `made-to-stick` only after the message is true and structurally clear.

Required output:
- customer is the protagonist; Urava/product is the guide;
- problem, plan, proof, CTA and success state are explicit;
- one core message/commander's intent;
- only the SUCCESs dimensions actually broken are strengthened;
- claims, proof, stakes and emotional framing remain evidence-backed.

Fail/route back: messaging cannot compensate for weak demand, bad positioning, bad offer, or bad economics. Route to the actual upstream bottleneck.

## Developer House routing

- **Product & Requirements:** Mom Test, offer, WTP, message truth.
- **Founder:** price, commercial commitment, material spend.
- **Observability:** channel economics, lead quality, conversion and experiment evidence.
- **Lead/Integration execution:** operational lead systems.
- **UI/UX:** website/message hierarchy and comprehension.
- **Devil's Advocate:** unsupported claims, bad assumptions, deceptive scarcity/urgency, framework overreach.

## Evidence loop

Every stage emits an `EVIDENCE.md` record. A stage may become reusable automation only after its input/output contract is stable enough to implement as a checklist, rubric, template or tool.
