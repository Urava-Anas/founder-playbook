# Urava Authority Contract

## Precedence

When a founder-playbook module conflicts with another source, apply this order:

1. Platform/system safety and law.
2. Explicit current founder instruction.
3. Urava locked governance and current executive source-of-truth.
4. Current project/architecture source-of-truth.
5. Verified operational evidence from canonical systems.
6. Urava Founder Intelligence adapters and validated evidence rules.
7. Upstream founder-playbook modules.
8. General model knowledge or community opinion.

A lower layer may inform a higher layer; it may not override it.

## Authority classes

Every recommendation must be classified before execution:

- **Auto-safe:** research, analysis, internal drafting, non-production experiments, reversible tests.
- **Founder-gated:** money/spend, pricing commitments, production state, credentials, legal/identity, ownership/access, destructive changes, high-impact external communications.
- **Observe-only:** useful framework insight without enough evidence to justify action.

## Commercial integrity

Frameworks about scarcity, urgency, persuasion, positioning or guarantees cannot authorize deceptive claims, fabricated proof, false scarcity, hidden material terms, or unsupported performance claims.

## Technical boundary

Business-book frameworks may influence product priority, messaging, discovery, offer design and experiments. They do not override security, data isolation, migration safety, exact-state verification, rollback, or provider-independence rules.

## Reality override

If an imported framework predicts X but repeated, well-measured Urava evidence shows Y, record the disagreement. Do not silently force the framework. Downgrade confidence, define the context where it still applies, and prefer the verified evidence for the same context.

## Source hygiene

Founder Playbook is MIT-licensed commentary/analysis. Underlying books remain separate copyrighted sources. Do not treat a distilled skill as equivalent to the complete book or represent it as verbatim source text.
